# Archive drawings of Gnejsen, plan 1 tr

The city's archive sheets for the building, as registered in the plan "Gnejsen 1 tr – hela planet, stadens ritningar"
(`gnejsen-1-tr-hela-planet-stade-424be4a373a46feb`). The TIFF files are in the maintainer's Google Drive (two copies
of the same set). All are black-and-white scans (CCITT G4 TIFF, about 300 dpi); no grey or higher-resolution original
exists in the Drive.

The other sheets in the Drive folder (series 02396731–02396871, 31311821–31312001, 18441131/51, 18122461/81,
12840491–12840521, 13103901, 12303811) are other floors, sections and installation drawings of the same building; they
are not yet indexed sheet by sheet.

**Dates matter.** A difference between two drawings can be a real change to the building, not an error (the kitchen
wall of 153 and the shower cabins changed after 1999). How the wall reader uses several drawings is in
`docs/wall-reader-plan.md` (stage W6).
