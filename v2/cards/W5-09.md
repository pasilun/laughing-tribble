Files: `v2/crates/render/src/svg.rs`, `sheet.rs` (and callers of `drawn_elements` inside the render crate).

Problem (spec owner, looking at the A3 PDF): on a sheet the windows are thin coloured lines that read as nothing, and colours do not belong on a permit drawing.

Do (D49): `drawn_elements` takes one more argument, `sheet: bool` (it is `pub(crate)`; `plan_svg` and `render` pass `false`, `sheet` passes `true`). When `sheet` is true:
- rooms: `fill="#ffffff"` (no tint).
- openings: the gap rectangle gets `stroke="#000000" stroke-width="{s/2}"` (`s` = the stroke argument); the line and the door path are `#000000`. A window gets a second line: the gap rectangle's centre line is replaced by two lines at one third and two thirds of the wall thickness (for a horizontal wall with gap y from lo to hi: y = lo + (hi − lo)/3 and y = lo + 2(hi − lo)/3, from start to end). Demolished openings keep `#c62828` dashed.
- fixtures and stubs: unchanged.
With `sheet` false the output is byte for byte what it is today.

Tests:
- sheet.rs (fixture A, A3): the sheet SVG contains no `#1f77d0`, no `#2a9d4b`, no `#8c4a00` and no `#eef4fb`; the element `O:Sovrum.south:1` has two `<line` (y1 = 4640 and y1 = 4740: the wall is y 4540…4840).
- svg.rs: `plan_svg` of fixture A equals its output before this card (an existing snapshot or string test pins it; if none does, assert it still contains `#1f77d0` and `#eef4fb`).
