Rules (D43):
- `All`: a plain clone.
- `Before` (the building today): remove walls and openings with phase `New`; walls and openings with phase `Demolish` stay and get phase `Existing` (drawn as ordinary walls). Remove rooms whose `room_phases` entry is `New` from `rooms` and `room_phases`.
- `After`: remove walls and openings with phase `Demolish`; `New` ones stay and get phase `Existing`. Remove rooms whose phase is `Demolish`. An opening whose wall was removed is removed too.
- `nodes`, `spaces` and `diagnostics` are copied unchanged.

CLI: `plan pdf … --phase all|before|after` (default all). The sheet is drawn from `phase_view(&resolved, view)`. The sheet title gets a suffix: before → ` – existing` (Sv: ` – före`), after → ` – proposed` (Sv: ` – efter`), all → none. MCP `export`: optional `phase` (`"all"` | `"before"` | `"after"`, default `"all"`), written to `<plan_id>.pdf`, `<plan_id>-before.pdf` or `<plan_id>-after.pdf`.

Tests:
- phase.rs, fixture `review-1` (wall `W:A.east|B.west` is demolish, the door `O:A.north:1` and the window `O:B.south:1` are new, room B is demolish):
  - Before: the wall is present with phase Existing; neither opening is present; rooms A and B present.
  - After: the wall is absent; both openings present with phase Existing; room B absent, room A present.
  - All: equals the input.
- CLI: `plan pdf fixtures/review-1.plan -o x.pdf --phase after` → exit 0; `--phase sideways` → exit 2.
