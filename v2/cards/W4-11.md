Do: `SheetOptions` gains `pub dims: bool` (existing literals: `false`, authorised mechanical edit; CLI `plan pdf` and MCP `export` pass `true` unless `--no-dims` / `"dims": false`).

When `dims` is true (D42):
1. **Space.** The fit test and the placement reserve 14 mm of paper on the north and west sides: `fits(n) = ew/n + 14 ≤ aw && eh/n + 14 ≤ ah`; `tx = 15 + 14 + (aw − 14 − ew·k)/2 − minx·k`; `ty` the same with ah, eh, miny. (With `dims` false nothing changes.)
2. **North chain.** Take every outer wall whose `between.0` side is North. Collect the west and east coordinates of those rooms' rectangles (`resolved.rooms`), sort, remove duplicates: ticks x₀ < x₁ < … If there are fewer than 2 ticks, draw nothing. In model units inside the plan group, with `u = n` (1 mm of paper):
   - chain line at `y = miny − 5u` from x₀ to x_last, stroke `#000000`, width `0.25u`;
   - at each tick a short line from `y − 1.5u` to `y + 1.5u`;
   - for each neighbouring pair with `(x_{i+1} − x_i)/n ≥ 6` (at least 6 mm on paper): the text `{x_{i+1} − x_i}` centred at (`(x_i + x_{i+1})/2`, `y − 2u`), font size `2.2u`, using the `text` helper;
   - a total line at `y = miny − 11u` from x₀ to x_last with end ticks and the text `{x_last − x₀}` centred above it at `y − 2u`. If there are exactly 2 ticks, the total line is left out.
3. **West chain.** The same with outer walls on the West side, the north and south coordinates of those rooms, on vertical lines at `x = minx − 5u` and `x = minx − 11u`; texts centred at (`x − 2u`, mid) and rotated: add `transform="rotate(-90 {cx} {cy})"` through the `extra` argument of `text`.
4. All of this goes in `<g id="dims">` at the end of the plan group.

Hand check, fixture A: north ticks 0, 1860, 1981, 3081, 3171, 5541 → segments 1860, 121, 1100, 90, 2370, total 5541. West ticks 0, 2360, 2450, 4540 → segments 2360, 90, 2090, total 4540.
