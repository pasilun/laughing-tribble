```
root = fresh dir under env!("CARGO_TARGET_TMPDIR"); copy fixtures lgh154-a.plan and lgh154-b.plan into it
child = Command::new(env!("CARGO_BIN_EXE_plan-mcp")).args(["--root", root]).stdin(piped).stdout(piped).spawn()
write these lines to stdin, each followed by "\n", then drop stdin (EOF):
  {"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":1,"method":"initialize","params":{"protocolVersion":"2025-06-18","capabilities":{},"clientInfo":{"name":"test","version":"0"}}}
  {"jsonrpc":"2.0","method":"notifications/initialized"}
  {"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":2,"method":"tools/list"}
  {"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":3,"method":"tools/call","params":{"name":"check","arguments":{"plan_id":"lgh154-b"}}}
  {"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":4,"method":"tools/call","params":{"name":"render","arguments":{"plan_id":"lgh154-a","max_tokens":800}}}
read stdout to the end; wait for the child, at most 20 s (poll try_wait with sleeps of 100 ms; kill and fail on timeout)
parse each stdout line as JSON; index the responses by "id" (they may arrive out of order)
```

- id 4: `result.content[0].type == "image"`, `mimeType == "image/png"`, `result.structuredContent.tokens <= 800`.
- the child exited with status 0.
