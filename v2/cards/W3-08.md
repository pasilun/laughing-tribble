Files: `v2/crates/core/src/build/openings.rs`.

Problem found by the audit: `door A.north at 500 width 900` and `door A.north at 900 width 900` are both accepted.

Do: at the end of `place_openings`, after `placed` is sorted by id: for every pair (a, b) with a before b in that order and `a.wall == b.wall`: `d = min(a.end, b.end) − max(a.start, b.start)`. If `d > 0`: push E016 (Error) with args a = a.id, b = b.id, d, wall; `refs: [a.id, b.id]`; `data: json!({"a": a.id, "b": b.id, "overlap": d, "wall": wall})`. Both openings stay in `placed`. (`start` and `end` are absolute along the wall, so this also catches a door on `A.east` against one on `B.west` in the same wall.)

Tests (openings.rs test module, same helpers as the existing tests):
- Room `A 3000 x 2000 source laser`; `door A.north at 500 width 900 hinge west opens A`; `door A.north at 900 width 900 hinge west opens A`: one E016, `Openings O:A.north:1 and O:A.north:2 overlap by 500 mm on wall W:A.north:outer.`
- Second door at 1400 instead (they touch): no E016.
- Fixture A: no E016.
