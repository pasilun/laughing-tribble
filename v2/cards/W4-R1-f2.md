Do: when a mark is shorter on screen than its label (fixture C's 1 mm conflict mark, and the 90 mm missing mark in a two-room plan), the label is placed on the mark's midpoint and hides the mark. The fix would place such a label beside the mark (offset to one side of the line) instead of on its midpoint. This changes W4-03 step 2 and the D38 rule about where labels go, so it is blocked until the spec owner decides the placement rule (offset distance, and which side).

Blocked (run 2026-11-10 build, reviewer W4-R1): decision needed from the spec owner: the exact rule for a label beside a short mark (side, offset in px). Nothing has been changed in the code.

Unblocked 2026-11-10 by the spec owner (rule committed to the repo, spec §15 D38 second amendment). File: `v2/crates/render/src/render.rs` or `marks.rs`, where the mark label requests are built (W4-03 step 2).
Rule: let `len_px` be the mark's length in px (its length in mm divided by `mm_per_px`). When `len_px < 40.0`, the label request's anchor is not the midpoint but the midpoint moved 20 px perpendicular to the mark: **east** (x + 20) for a vertical mark (from.x == to.x), **north** (y − 20) for a horizontal mark. Marks of 40 px or longer are unchanged. Nothing else about label placement changes.
Tests: fixture C through `resolve_draft` and `conflict_marks`, rendered at 1600 tokens (mm_per_px 6): the label with id `conflict:1` has an anchor 20 px east of the mark's midpoint in px. Fixture B's label anchor is still the midpoint (its mark is about 748 px long).
