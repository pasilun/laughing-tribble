Files: `v2/crates/render/src/svg.rs` (new function `fixture_element`, called from `drawn_elements` after the walls and before the openings); `render.rs` (label requests).

Do: each fixture is `<g class="fixture" data-id="{id}" data-kind="{kind word}">…</g>`. With the rectangle (x0, y0, x1, y1), w = x1 − x0, h = y1 − y0, `s` = the stroke argument (the one openings get), colour `#333333`, all shapes `fill="#ffffff" stroke="#333333" stroke-width="{s/2}"` unless said otherwise:
- every kind: the rectangle itself.
- stove: plus four circles, radius `min(w, h)/8`, centred at the quarter points (x0 + w/4, y0 + h/4), (x0 + 3w/4, y0 + h/4), (x0 + w/4, y0 + 3h/4), (x0 + 3w/4, y0 + 3h/4), `fill="none"`.
- sink: plus an inner rectangle inset by `min(w, h)/6` on every side, `fill="none"`.
- wc: plus an ellipse centred in the rectangle with rx = 0.35·w and ry = 0.35·h, `fill="none"`.
- shower: the rectangle is dashed (`stroke-dasharray="{2·s} {2·s}"`), plus a circle of radius `min(w, h)/10` at the centre.
- bath: plus an inner rectangle inset by `min(w, h)/8` with `rx="{min(w, h)/4}"`, `fill="none"`.
- radiator: the rectangle filled `#333333`.
- exhaust: plus the two diagonals of the rectangle.
- plug: the rectangle, and the text `P` centred in it (font size `0.8·min(w, h)`, the `text` helper).
- shaft: plus the two diagonals. box, kitchen, balcony: the rectangle only (balcony `fill="none"`).
- a fixture with a `label` gets that text centred in the rectangle, font size = the area font of the `fonts` argument (the plug's `P` is replaced by the label).
- phase Demolish: every stroke of the fixture is `#c62828` and dashed `{4·s} {3·s}`; phase New: strokes `#1a1a1a` with width `s`.
`render` (labels view): fixtures get a label request with their id, priority 2, like openings. The plan view does not label them.
