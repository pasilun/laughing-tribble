Problem (spec owner, A3 PDF of fixture A): in Hall (1100 × 1113 mm) the clear-size text is wider than the room, and the WC door's swing runs through the name.

Do (D49), on sheets only (n = the scale denominator):
1. **Clear-size text** is printed only when the room is at least 25 mm wide and 14 mm deep on paper: `rw/n ≥ 25 && rd/n ≥ 14`.
2. **Label position.** For each room that gets a name, collect the swing boxes of the hinged doors whose leaf swings into that room: the bounding box of the three path points H, L, J of W3-13 (target room = this room). The label block's box, centred on a candidate position (px, py): width = the largest of `0.6 · font · characters` over its lines (name at font 3.5n, area at 2.5n, and the clear-size line at 2.5n when rule 1 prints it), height = 11n. Try these positions in order and take the first whose box overlaps no swing box (touching edges is not overlapping) and lies inside the room rectangle: the centre (cx, cy); then (cx, cy + rd/4); (cx, cy − rd/4); (cx + rw/4, cy); (cx − rw/4, cy). If none is free, use the centre. The name, area and clear-size texts keep their offsets from the position and move together.
   To do this, give `svg::room` an optional label centre (`Option<(f64, f64)>`, `None` = the rectangle's centre as today) and have the sheet pass it; `plan_svg` and `render` pass `None` and are unchanged.
