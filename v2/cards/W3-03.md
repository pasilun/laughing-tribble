Files: `v2/crates/core/src/resolved.rs`.

Problem found by the audit. This plan:
```
plan "p"
default_wall 90 source assumed
default_outer_wall 300 source assumed

room A 2000 x ? source laser
room B 1500 x 1000 source laser
  east_of A gap
  align north A
```
gives the right W001 (`A depth unknown; measure A.north to A.south`) but also draws A with north = south = 0, area 0, and reports `I010 Wall W:A.north|A.south is 0 mm`.

Rule (D24): after both axes are solved and before `built_walls`, a room is **unplaced** when, on some axis, its two faces on that axis are not in the same component (west/east on x, north/south on y). Remove all coordinates of an unplaced room's faces on that axis from `coords` (both faces). Everything downstream already skips a room with a missing coordinate (`facing_faces`, `rooms`, `room_areas`, `place_openings`), so the room gets no rectangle, no walls, no area and no openings. Its W001 stays.

How: in the per-axis loop of `resolve` you have `comps` (the components of that axis; `Component.faces: Vec<FaceId>` with `room` and `side`). For each room in `plan.order`, find the index of the component holding each of its two faces on this axis; if they differ, remember (room, axis). After the loop (and after the first `has_error` return), remove those keys from `coords.x` / `coords.y`. Look at how the map keys (`FaceKey`) are built in `crates/core/src/build/walls.rs` (`face_key`) and in `rooms()` in resolved.rs, and build them the same way.

A room that has sizes but is not tied to the others (the plain "Not tied" W001) is **not** unplaced by this card: it stays where it is drawn today.
