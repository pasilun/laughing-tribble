Do: `scan` (action "candidates") also offers, as `kind: "extend"` with the wall's id in `wall`, a wall of the plan that the drawing carries further than the plan does. Its `op` is an `update` of the wall's ends (`edit` keeps the wall's openings in place when the a-end moves). The drawn wall is followed across gaps no wider than a doorway (1200 mm), since the plan draws a wall with a doorway as one wall with an opening. An extended end that stops just short of a crossing wall is joined to that wall's centre line. An extension never goes past a wall the line already ends against (a T-joint or a corner: beyond lies a stair, another room or the next flat) nor into the next wall of the plan on the same line. Candidates now carry `kind` (`"add"` or `"extend"`); the existing `add` candidates are unchanged. Why: in the hand trial on the cellar drawing, walls traced 2 m short were never offered, because the candidates skip anything that overlaps a wall the plan has.
